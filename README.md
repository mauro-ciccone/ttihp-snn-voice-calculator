# ttihp-snn-voice-calculator

- [Read the documentation for project](docs/info.md)

## What is it?

A voice powered addition/subtraction calculator that recognizes the keywords: 1,2,3,plus,minus

## How does it work

### Input
The Uio[0] is the input for a PDM-Microphone, the bit-stream generated by it is feed into a 6 channel silicon cochlea that converts the bit-stream into distinct frequency spikes using 6 lif-neurons with heterogeneous thresholds&leak-rates.

### Compute
The spikes generated by the silicon cochlea are feed into the hidden layer consisting of 80 completely spatial unrolled lif-neurons with heterogeneous thresholds, betas & register-sizes depending on the required integer/fractional-bits. They are connected back into the Hidden-layer through a complete recursive-synapse-layer and to the 6 output-neurons, through a complete output-synapse-layer. These lif-neurons use pure power of 2 based betas, such that the leak can be accurately implemented using bit-shift subtractors. The fractional-bit-count is decided by the deepest shift occurring in the beta for each neuron, while the integer-bit-count is decided by the threshold & largest incoming spike-weight to prevent overflow. The spike-weights are purely integer based and consist of the 75% largest weight-synapses scaled to signed 8bit integers. The smallest 25% synapses were pruned. To prevent the need for a ~86-Adder large Adder-tree for each hidden-neuron, we use a or-gate based approximate summation for input spikes. This works with almost 0% information loss, since we used a custom compiler that groups the spikes with minimal bitwise weights overlap and maximal temporal spike diffrence into a single or-gate. Important here is to seperate positive and negative weights such that 2's complement can be converted into the absolute value to prevent massive overlap in the negative weights. The negative-or-gate outputs are summed together and subtracted from the mambrane potential, while the positive weights are added to it.
